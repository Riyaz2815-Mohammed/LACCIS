NOW, BOTH PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS: 
1. Purpose: The purpose of this Agreement is to define the terms and conditions under 
which the Referrer shall identify and introduce potential enterprise clients (”Leads” or 
“Referred Clients”) to the Solution Provider for the purchase or subscription of its 
Product/Services . TYN shall act as a Sell Through Agent between the Client and the 
Solution Provider, assisting in introductions, insights on commercial discussions, 
evaluation processes, and deal closure, while the Solution Provider remains the 
seller of record and invoices the Client directly 
 
2. Term: This Agreement shall commence & remain in force until 31/12/2028 subject to 
periodical renewals with mutual written consent between the Parties in writing.

3. Referrer’s Responsibilities 
The Referrer agrees to: 
a)​ Featuring the Solution Provider on NiFo, the referrer’s proprietary platform. 
b)​ Introducing the Solution Provider to potential enterprise clients. 
c)​ Facilitating connections with the appropriate stakeholders. 
d)​ Sharing insights on enterprise processes and best practices. 
e)​ Supporting technical discussions and evaluations as required. 
 
4. Solution Provider’s Responsibilities 
The Solution provider agrees to: 
a)​ Follow up with the Referred Leads and, if applicable, provide necessary information or 
assistance for the customer’s purchase of the Product or services. 
b)​ Own and drive all commercial discussions, negotiations, and final agreements with 
enterprise clients. 
c)​ Notify the Referrer of any successful sales that result from a referral. 
 
5. Referral Fee: 
The Solution Provider agrees to pay the Referrer a referral fee for successful client 
introductions that lead to commercial engagements, as outlined below: 
 
a)​ Straight Purchase Deals:  
i.​ The Solution Provider agrees to pay a referral fee of 10% of the total revenue 
received (excluding government taxes) from the referred client to TYN, or 
₹10,00,000 (Rupees Ten Lakhs Only), whichever is lower, per deal. 
ii.​ The referral fee will be paid within 10 working days after the Solution Provider 
receives Purchase Order (“PO”) from the Referred Client. 
b)​ Subscription-Based Deals:  
i.​ The Solution Provider agrees to pay a referral fee of 10% of the invoiced amount to 
TYN (excluding government taxes) for each monthly billing cycle for a period of 12 
consecutive months from the date of the first invoice of each deal raised to the 
Referred Client. The referral fee shall be paid within seven (7) working days from the 
date of each invoice raised to the Referred Client. 
c)​ Transaction-Based Deals:  
i.​ The Solution Provider agrees to pay a referral fee of 10% of the total monthly 
invoice amount to TYN (excluding government taxes), payable at the end of each 
month, for a period of 12 consecutive months from the date of the first invoice of 
each transaction that is raised by the Solution Provider to the Referred Client.  
ii.​ The referral fee shall be paid within seven (7) working days from the date of each 
invoice raised to the Referred Client. Any chargebacks, adjustments, or balance 
reconciliations shall be accounted for and settled after the 12 month period for each 
deal. 
d)​ The Solution Provider agrees to pay the referral fee for any deal closures that occur 
within one year from the date the lead is generated.  
e)​ The Solution Provider shall share copies of all invoices and payment receipts related to 
referred clients within 3 business days of issuance. The Referrer shall have the right to 
audit such records, upon reasonable notice, to verify accuracy of referral fee payments.

6. Joint Notification Clause: The solution provider agrees to share a copy of 
every invoice (with a date/timestamp) and every payment confirmation (proof of 
bank deposit) with TYN within 48 hours of sending/receiving. Failure to do so is a 
material breach of the agreement, triggering penalties and audit rights. 
7. Visibility & Reporting Rights: The Solution Provider agrees to provide TYN 
with reasonable visibility into:   
a)​ Deal stage progress and status updates;   
b)​ Final commercial terms agreed with Client;   
c)​ Invoices and payment receipts relating to the Solution for calculation of 
Referral Fee ;   
d)​ Any contract amendments, renewal, add-on or expansion deals relating to 
the Referred  Client within 12 months from deal closure, or until the 
Referral Fee is due 
For the avoidance of doubt, the Parties acknowledge that the solution provider 
expressly requires that all invoices relating to a referred deal shall be issued using 
a three-party invoice template, and TYN shall have a contractual right to receive 
payment data and/or exercise control over the payment flow for the purpose of 
verifying Referral Fee  entitlement and enforcing payment obligations. 
8. Non-Circumvention: 
a)​ The Solution Provider hereby agrees and undertakes that it shall not, directly or 
indirectly, attempt to circumvent, avoid, bypass, or obviate the Referrer’s role by 
contacting, engaging, or transacting with any Lead, Referred Client, or party 
introduced by the Referrer, whether for the same or a similar product or service, 
without routing such engagement through the Referrer.  
b)​ This obligation shall remain in force during the term of this Agreement and for a 
period of two (2) years following its termination or expiry.  
c)​ In the event of any such circumvention or breach, the Referrer shall be entitled to 
claim and recover full referral fees, loss of business opportunity, and consequential 
damages, in addition to any other remedies available under law. 
9. Exclusivity   
a)​ The Solution Provider agrees that for any Lead and/or Referred Client introduced 
by TYN, TYN shall have exclusive rights to facilitate  all discussions with such Lead 
and/or Referred Client for 12 months from the date of introduction.   
i.​ During this period, the Solution Provider shall not directly or indirectly engage 
with the lead and/or Referred Client by themselves or using any other 
intermediary to approach or transact with the same Referred Client for similar or 
competing services.   
ii.​ Any direct engagement without TYN’s prior written consent constitutes a material 
breach, entitling TYN to the full referral fee for the engagement and damages for 
lost business and goodwill.    
10. Limitation of liability: In no event shall the referrer or any of its directors, officers, 
employees, or agents be liable for any consequential, indirect, direct, exemplary, special 
punitive, or incidental damages, arising from or relating to this Agreement and any 
agreement entered between Solution Provider and any party introduced by the Referrer.

12. Entire Agreement: This Agreement contains the entire agreement between the Parties, 
and supersedes any prior written or oral agreements between them concerning the subject 
matter contained herein. If any provision of this Agreement shall be held invalid or 
unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remaining provisions hereof shall 
continue to be fully effective.  
13. Notices: All notices, requests, or other communications under this Agreement shall be 
sent by the Parties to each other at the addresses specified in this Agreement, by registered 
post or courier. 
14. Amendments: All amendments or modifications to this Agreement shall be made in 
writing and signed by all Parties. 
15. Governing Law / Jurisdiction / Dispute Resolution: This Agreement shall be governed 
by Indian laws. In case of disputes, either Party shall approach the competent courts at 
Chennai, which shall have the sole and exclusive jurisdiction to determine the disputes.
